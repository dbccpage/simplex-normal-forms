# Anomaly Basis

Status: Definitional / Proof obligation.

This is the minimal basis for the cleaned taxonomy. New proposed normal forms or contracts should map to one or more of these anomalies instead of inventing a new category.

| Anomaly | Definition | Typical category |
|---|---|---|
| boundary violation | write/event intersects an undeclared or unrepairable observer boundary | change normal form |
| missing evidence | required finite packet fields are absent or not replayable | evidence form |
| gauge drift | representation changes authority-relevant content without a witness | authority contract / experimental extension |
| observer overreach | an observer claims facts outside its declared visibility or comparison envelope | observer contract |
| authority laundering | available/valid/certified data is presented as authorized | authority contract |
| orphan certificate | certificate record exists without an active verifier replaying it | certificate form |
| loss-ledger missing | transport erases distinctions without `Loss(F)` | ledger form |
| stale authority | a read is presented as current without freshness or invalidation state | epistemic normal form |
| detached provenance | output has no replayable source/transform chain | evidence form |
| context smuggling | user, retrieval, tool, and policy layers are collapsed without typed boundaries | observer contract |
| non-replayable process | action or derivation cannot be reconstructed from logs and packet state | evidence form |
| policy interference | policy domains are mixed or bypassed during transport/repair | authority contract |
| budget truncation | approximation, token, time, cost, or uncertainty budget is silently exceeded | ledger form |
| ontology laundering | finite observer packets are promoted into larger ontological claims without declared transport or authority boundary | experimental observer contract |
| illicit global state | local observer packets are promoted into one global state without gluing/comparison witness | experimental observer contract |
| hidden-state smuggling | hidden variables or mechanisms are used without projection, dynamics, and nonlocality ledgers | experimental observer contract |
| unmeasured property laundering | pre-measurement representation is promoted into measured authority without terminal event | experimental observer contract |
| branch ontology laundering | branch decomposition is treated as ontology without observer localization, decoherence, measure, and comparison policy | experimental observer contract |
| information ontology laundering | bits or distinctions are treated as physical authority without encoding, observer, record, and realization map | experimental observer contract |

Non-claims: does not replace Codd / relational theory; does not claim all data problems are normal forms; does not claim certification equals authorization; does not claim metadata creates authority; does not require category theory for implementation; does not prove all experimental regimes.
