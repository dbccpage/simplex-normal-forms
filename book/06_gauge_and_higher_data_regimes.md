# Gauge and Higher Data Regimes

Status: Experimental conjecture / Domain extension.

This chapter is the quarantine boundary. Experimental data regimes are allowed as operational conjectures, not as core normal forms.

## Protected Term Rule

NF is a protected term. A proposal may be called a normal form only if it declares:

1. the representation normalized
2. the anomaly excluded
3. the finite packet that checks it
4. the verifier or executable check
5. the terminal routes
6. the implementation failure it prevents

If it cannot answer all six, it is not an NF. It is a contract, packet, ledger, regime, conjecture, or domain extension.

## Quarantine Policy

The database program should not absorb physics or every application domain. It should export a finite-authority grammar that physics, legal, market, satellite, condensed-matter, and other applications may instantiate later.

The following do not enter the core taxonomy:

- Bell NF
- Bohm NF
- Copenhagen NF
- DeWitt-Everett NF
- Wheeler NF
- UnGodLy NF
- Quantum NF
- Market NF

## Observer-Ontology Patterns

These are contracts for how a system is allowed to promote local observer packets into claims about hidden state, measurement, branch structure, or information ontology.

They are not physics claims. They do not say Bohm is true, Copenhagen is true, Everett is true, Bell settles ontology, or Wheeler's information ontology is physically established.

Serious thesis:

> Quantum interpretations are representation regimes. Their danger is not that they are wrong; it is that they promote finite observer packets into ontological claims without declaring the transport, loss, branch, hidden-state, or realization ledger.

Patterns:

| Pattern | Blocks |
|---|---|
| BellCNF observer contract | local packets promoted into illicit global state |
| Bohm hidden-state ledger | hidden variables without projection/dynamics/nonlocality ledger |
| Copenhagen measurement boundary | unmeasured properties promoted into measurement authority |
| DeWitt-Everett branch ledger | branch decomposition promoted into unledgered ontology |
| Wheeler realization boundary | bits/information promoted into physical authority without realization map |

BellCNF is intentionally not named `BCNF_qm`; BCNF is reserved for Boyce-Codd Normal Form in relational contexts.
